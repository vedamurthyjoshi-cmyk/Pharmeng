💡 What you are seeing

High-pressure gas (air or steam) enters through nozzles tangentially around the oval loop. Drug particles are fed in and accelerate to near-sonic velocity. Particles collide with each other (inter-particle impact) — no metal contact. Small particles (<5 µm) exit through the central outlet by centrifugal classification; large particles remain in the loop for further grinding.

🔑 The Fluid Energy Mill is the only commonly used mill that produces no heat (gas expands on entering → cools down) — ideal for thermolabile drugs including steroids and antibiotics for inhalation therapy.

Principle

The Fluid Energy Mill (Microniser/Jet Mill) uses high-velocity compressed gas (air or steam at 7–10 bar) to accelerate particles to near-sonic speed inside an oval or circular loop. Particles collide with each other at high energy → inter-particle impact → size reduction. No grinding media contact — no metal contamination. Size classification is built in: centrifugal force keeps large particles circulating in the loop; small particles (1–10 µm) migrate to the centre and exit through the product outlet.

Construction

  • Loop (racetrack): Oval or circular flattened tube, 25–200 mm diameter
  • Gas jets: Multiple nozzles positioned tangentially around the loop; high-pressure gas creates circulating vortex
  • Feed inlet: Drug powder fed into the high-velocity gas stream through a Venturi feed nozzle
  • Product outlet: Central port — fine particles exit by inertial classification
  • Gas supply: Air compressor (7–10 bar) or steam; gas is filtered before entry (no contamination)

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Inhalation drugs: Salbutamol, budesonide, fluticasone — must be 1–5 µm for deep lung deposition; fluid energy mill is the standard method
  • Steroids: Prednisolone, beclomethasone — thermolabile; only jet milling safe
  • Antibiotics: Griseofulvin, amphotericin B — heat-sensitive; jet milled
  • BCS Class II drugs: Improving dissolution by particle size reduction without heat degradation
🔑 Particle size range: 1–10 µm (micronised). For inhalation therapy (MDI, DPI), 1–5 µm particles required to reach alveoli. Particles > 10 µm deposit in throat; < 0.5 µm exhaled without deposition.

Advantages and Disadvantages

  • ✅ No heat generation (Joule-Thomson cooling as gas expands) — ideal for thermolabile drugs
  • ✅ No metal contamination — no grinding media contact
  • ✅ Produces very fine particles (1–10 µm) — finest of all common mills
  • ✅ Self-classifying — no separate sieving needed
  • ✅ Sterile operation possible (filtered compressed air)
  • ❌ High energy cost (compressed gas)
  • ❌ Low throughput — not for large volume production
  • ❌ Not suitable for waxy, sticky, or moisture-sensitive materials
Q1. The unique advantage of the Fluid Energy Mill (Jet Mill) over Ball Mill and Hammer Mill is:
✅ Joule-Thomson effect: when compressed gas (7–10 bar) expands through nozzles → temperature drops (cooling). Net result: the milling zone is actually cooled by the expanding gas — opposite of Ball/Hammer mills which heat the product. This makes jet milling the standard method for heat-sensitive inhalation drugs (steroids, bronchodilators) where particle size must be 1–5 µm.
Q2. Why must salbutamol (albuterol) for MDI inhalers be micronised to 1–5 µm using a fluid energy mill?
✅ Aerodynamic diameter determines deposition site in lungs: > 10 µm → upper airways/throat (inertial impaction); 5–10 µm → large bronchi; 1–5 µm → small bronchi and alveoli (target for bronchodilators and inhaled steroids); < 0.5 µm → exhaled. Fluid energy mill achieves 1–5 µm range without heat → no degradation of thermolabile steroid or beta-agonist. This is why jet milling is THE standard process for inhalation API manufacturing.
Q3. Size classification in the Fluid Energy Mill is achieved by:
✅ Built-in classification: the circulating vortex inside the oval loop creates centrifugal force. Larger particles (higher mass) experience greater centrifugal force → stay in outer circulation → continue grinding. Smaller particles (lower mass) → gas drag force > centrifugal force → migrate toward centre → exit through product outlet. This automatic size selection means no separate sieving is needed after fluid energy milling.
