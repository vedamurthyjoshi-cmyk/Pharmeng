Cooling Coil Dehumidifier — Moisture Removal from Air

Cooling Coil Dehumidification

Moist warm air is passed over cold coils (chilled water at 6–12°C). When air temperature drops below its dew point, water vapour condenses on the coil surface and drains away.

Process Steps

  • Step 1 — Sensible cooling: Air temperature falls but RH rises (no condensation yet)
  • Step 2 — Dew point reached: Air at 100% RH; condensation begins on coil fins
  • Step 3 — Latent cooling: Moisture condenses; absolute humidity drops
  • Step 4 — Drain: Condensate collects in drip tray and drains away
  • Step 5 — Reheat: Cool dry air re-heated to supply temperature (optional)

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Controlling RH in tablet coating suites (30–40% RH needed)
  • Maintaining hygroscopic powder stability in granulation area
  • Dew point control in freeze-drying anteroom
  • Air handling unit (AHU) conditioning coil
