Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs)

AGVs are driverless battery-powered vehicles that transport materials along fixed or programmable routes within pharmaceutical manufacturing facilities. They follow magnetic tape, laser targets, or GPS-like indoor navigation.

Navigation Methods

  • Magnetic tape guidance: vehicle follows embedded tape; simple, rigid routes
  • Laser triangulation: laser scans reflectors on walls; flexible routing
  • Natural navigation: SLAM (simultaneous localisation and mapping); no markers needed
  • Vision-based: cameras + AI; most advanced

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Moving IBCs between granulation and compression
  • Transporting sterilised goods to aseptic filling
  • Cold-store to production line material transfer
  • WIP (work-in-progress) pallet movement
  • Finished goods transfer to warehouse

Advantages

  • 24/7 operation; no fatigue or human error
  • Reduces personnel in classified areas
  • Full electronic audit trail of material movements
  • Predictable, repeatable, contamination-free transport
