Bernoulli's Theorem

For steady, incompressible flow along a streamline: P + ½ρv² + ρgh = constant

Where P = static pressure, ρ = fluid density, v = velocity, g = gravity, h = height.

Key Principles

  • At a constriction (narrow section), velocity increases and pressure drops
  • Total energy per unit volume remains constant along a streamline
  • Continuity equation: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (wider pipe → slower flow)
  • Applications: Venturi meter, orifice meter, rotameter, spray atomisers
  • Assumptions: inviscid, incompressible, steady, along a streamline

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Venturi mixer: low-pressure zone draws in second fluid
  • Spray drying: high-velocity air atomises liquid feed
  • Fluid bed dryer: fluidisation velocity calculated from Bernoulli principles
  • IV flow regulation: pressure–velocity balance in infusion sets
