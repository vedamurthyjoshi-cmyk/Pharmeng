💡 What you are seeing

The pestle (heavy runner) rotates on a fixed vertical axis while also orbiting around the mortar bowl. Material is crushed between the flat face of the pestle and the flat bottom of the mortar by compression and attrition. This is essentially a mechanised mortar-and-pestle — the traditional pharmaceutical preparation tool.

🔑 The End Runner Mill is the mechanical equivalent of the pharmacist's mortar and pestle. It works by the flat end (face) of the pestle pressing and sliding on the flat mortar floor — hence "End Runner" (runs on its end/face).

Principle and Comparison with Edge Runner

The End Runner Mill works on the same principle as the Edge Runner Mill — compression and attrition — but with a different geometry:

  • Edge Runner Mill: Cylindrical wheels roll on their curved edge/circumference on a flat pan → contact line is along the cylinder length
  • End Runner Mill: Cylindrical pestle runs on its flat circular end/face on the mortar floor → contact area is the entire pestle face → higher contact area per unit → more compression per unit area → finer grinding
🔑 Key difference: End Runner produces finer particles than Edge Runner because the flat-face contact gives higher grinding pressure per unit area. End Runner is closer in design to a mortar and pestle — same principle scaled up mechanically.

Construction

  • Mortar: Heavy cast iron or granite flat-bottomed bowl; fixed in position
  • Pestle (runner): Cylindrical; runs on its flat end face on mortar floor; rotates about its own vertical axis AND orbits around the mortar centre
  • Drive: Motor drives the orbital motion via eccentric cam or crank mechanism; 20–60 rpm
  • Scraper: Pushes material back under the pestle face

Applications and Comparison

  • Grinding of soft materials — sugar, lactose, chemical compounds
  • Preparation of pill masses — traditional pharmaceutical use
  • Grinding paints, pigments, and pastes for pharmaceutical preparations
  • Handles moist and pasty materials better than hammer or ball mill
Q1. The End Runner Mill is described as a mechanised version of which traditional pharmaceutical instrument?
✅ Mortar and pestle principle: pharmacist places material in mortar → applies pestle with downward pressure while rotating → compression + attrition grinds particles. End Runner Mill mechanises this: the pestle (runner) is driven by a motor to orbit around the mortar while rotating on its own axis → same compression + attrition action → continuous and much more powerful than manual mortar and pestle. Historical importance: End Runner was the first mechanised pharmaceutical size reduction equipment in 19th century pharmacy.
Q2. The key geometric difference between an End Runner Mill and an Edge Runner Mill is:
✅ Contact geometry: End Runner (flat face on flat floor) = area contact → high pressure over large area → fine grinding. Edge Runner (curved wheel on flat pan) = line contact → very high pressure concentrated on a line → also fine grinding but with more lateral shear. Both use compression + attrition. End Runner is suited for finer, more controlled grinding. Edge Runner gives more lateral shear — better for mixing while grinding (ointments).
