Pharmaceutical HVAC — Cleanroom Air Handling System

Pharmaceutical HVAC

Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning systems in pharmaceutical manufacturing maintain cleanliness class, temperature, humidity, and pressure differentials to prevent contamination.

Air Handling Unit (AHU) Components

  • Pre-filter (G4): removes coarse particles >10 µm; protects downstream filters
  • Cooling coil: chilled water; dehumidifies and cools incoming air
  • Heating coil: steam or hot water; conditions air to set temperature
  • Supply fan: creates positive pressure; delivers treated air to rooms
  • HEPA filter: removes ≥99.997% of 0.3 µm particles; final filtration

Cleanroom Classes (ISO / EU GMP)

  • Grade A (ISO 5): aseptic filling; ≤3,520 particles/m³ ≥0.5µm
  • Grade B (ISO 7): background for Grade A; sterile preparations
  • Grade C (ISO 8): less critical sterile steps
  • Grade D (ISO 9): primary packaging, general manufacturing

Pressure Cascade

Higher cleanliness grades maintained at higher pressure — air flows outward from clean to less-clean areas, preventing ingress of contamination.
