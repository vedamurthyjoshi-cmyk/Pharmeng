What you are seeing

A perforated basket (bowl) lined with filter cloth rotates at 600-1800 rpm. Slurry (crystal suspension) is fed in from the top. Centrifugal force drives liquid through the filter cloth and basket perforations as filtrate. Solids (crystals) are retained as a cake on the filter cloth. The cake can be washed in-situ with fresh solvent, then discharged by a plough blade.

Key Point: The Basket Centrifuge is essentially a centrifugal filter. It uses centrifugal force (500-1500xg) instead of vacuum to drive filtrate through the filter medium -- producing drier cake in less time than vacuum filtration.

Basket Centrifuge -- Pharmaceutical Importance

  • Basket: Perforated SS bowl (600-1200mm dia) lined with filter cloth (woven SS or synthetic)
  • Speed: 600-1800 rpm generating 300-1500xg (much lower than tubular bowl)
  • Cycle: Feed (fill) >> Spin (separate) >> Wash (solvent wash in-situ) >> Dry spin >> Discharge (plough blade)
  • GMP advantage: Fully contained; CIP compatible; in-situ wash avoids transfer; PLC controlled for reproducibility
  • Pharmaceutical use: Isolation of API crystals after crystallisation -- standard operation in API manufacturing
Key Fact: In-situ washing in the basket centrifuge purifies the API cake without any transfer step. Impurities in mother liquor are washed out by fresh solvent while the cake stays in the basket -- minimising contamination risk and yield loss.
Q1. The Basket Centrifuge is preferred for isolating pharmaceutical API crystals because:
Correct. The in-situ wash cycle: after spinning off mother liquor, fresh solvent is sprayed onto the cake while it spins -- solvent displaces residual mother liquor (which contains impurities and by-products) -- then spin-dried again. This can be repeated 2-3 times. All in the same contained vessel -- no open transfers.
Q2. The cake formed in a Basket Centrifuge is drier than from a vacuum filter because:
Correct. Vacuum filter maximum driving force is limited by atmospheric pressure (approximately 0.9 bar). Basket centrifuge at 1200 rpm with 0.5m radius generates approximately 2 bar equivalent pressure at the basket wall -- twice the vacuum filtration force -- significantly drier cake -- less drying energy needed afterwards.
