What you are seeing

A perforated basket (bowl) with filter cloth lining rotates at 600–1800 rpm. Slurry is fed in. Centrifugal force drives liquid through the filter cloth and basket perforations (filtrate). Solids are retained as a cake on the filter cloth. Cake can be washed in situ then discharged by a plough blade.

The Basket Centrifuge is essentially a centrifugal filter — it uses centrifugal force instead of vacuum or pressure to drive filtrate through the filter medium. This gives faster filtration than Buchner funnels and drier cake than most pressure filters.

Q1. The Basket Centrifuge produces drier cake than a vacuum filter because:
✅ Centrifugal vs vacuum filtration driving force: vacuum filter maximum ΔP = 0.8–0.9 bar (limited by atmospheric pressure). Basket centrifuge: centrifugal pressure at basket wall = ρω²r²/2. At 1200 rpm with 0.5 m radius basket: pressure = 1000 × (2π×1200/60)² × 0.5² / 2 = 197,000 Pa ≈ 2 bar — twice the vacuum filtration driving force → significantly drier cake → less drying needed → energy saving.
Q2. In pharmaceutical API manufacturing, the Basket Centrifuge is preferred for:
✅ GMP advantages of basket centrifuge for API isolation: (1) Contained operation — reduces operator exposure to potent API dust; (2) In-situ wash — purifies cake without transferring it; (3) CIP (clean-in-place) compatible — spray nozzles wash basket and bowl; (4) Gentle discharge by plough — minimal crystal breakage; (5) Automated operation — PLC control of speed profile, wash timing, discharge — minimises operator intervention and ensures reproducibility.
