What you are seeing

The cylindrical bowl rotates at high speed (15,000–50,000 rpm). Feed enters from the bottom. Centrifugal force (up to 62,000×g) throws dense particles or heavy liquid phase to the bowl wall. Light phase overflows at top. Solids collected manually when bowl is stopped.

The Tubular Bowl Centrifuge generates centrifugal force of 13,000–62,000×g — far greater than any other pharmaceutical centrifuge. This extremely high force separates very fine particles (< 1 µm) and clarifies solutions that cannot be filtered.

The Tubular Bowl Centrifuge generates centrifugal force of 13,000–62,000×g — far greater than any other pharmaceutical centrifuge. This extremely high force separates very fine particles (< 1 µm) and clarifies solutions that cannot be filtered.
Q1. The Tubular Bowl Centrifuge is used for pharmaceutical clarification rather than batch filtration because:
✅ Separation by centrifugal force: F_centrifugal = mω²r where ω = angular velocity, r = radius. At 50,000 rpm with 50 mm radius: g-force = (2π×50000/60)²×0.025/9.81 = 34,600×g. This force is 34,600 times stronger than gravity — can sediment particles that would take years to settle by gravity. Applications: virus concentration for vaccines; plasma protein fractionation; bacteria harvesting from fermentation broth.
Q2. Solids recovery from a Tubular Bowl Centrifuge requires:
✅ Manual discharge limitation: solids accumulate on bowl wall during operation → bowl must be decelerated → stopped → opened → scraped manually → reassembled → re-accelerated. This batch cycle means: low throughput for heavily-loaded feeds; labour intensive; contamination risk during bowl opening. Alternative for continuous solids discharge: Disc Centrifuge (self-cleaning nozzle type) or Basket Centrifuge.
