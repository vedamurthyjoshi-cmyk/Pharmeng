Simple Distillation

Simple distillation separates liquids based on differences in boiling point. The mixture is heated; the more volatile component vaporises first, travels through the condenser where it is cooled and condensed back to liquid, and is collected as the distillate.

  • Principle: Differential vapour pressure at a given temperature
  • Suitable for: Mixtures with boiling point difference >25 °C, or solid-liquid separation
  • Components: Round-bottom flask, Liebig condenser, thermometer, receiver flask
  • Pharmaceutical use: Purification of solvents, preparation of distilled water, essential oil isolation
  • Limitation: Cannot separate close-boiling mixtures efficiently
