Belt Conveyor — Continuous Material Transfer

Belt Conveyor

A continuous flexible belt stretched between two pulleys conveys solid materials horizontally or at a slight incline. The belt is supported by idler rollers underneath.

Key Components

  • Drive pulley: powered; at discharge end; moves belt
  • Tail pulley: at feed end; maintains belt tension
  • Idler rollers: support belt between pulleys; reduce sag
  • Belt: rubber, PVC, or stainless steel mesh; continuous loop
  • Take-up device: adjusts belt tension automatically

Capacity

Throughput = Belt width × Material depth × Belt speed × Bulk density

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Moving tablet bottles on packaging lines
  • Transporting filled ampoules through inspection tunnel
  • Conveying blister packs to cartoning machine
  • Feeding granules from dryer to mill
