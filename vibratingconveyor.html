Vibrating Conveyor — Oscillating Trough Transport

Vibrating Conveyor

A trough mounted on leaf springs or rubber isolators is vibrated at an angle by an eccentric drive or electromagnetic vibrator. Material moves forward by micro-hops due to asymmetric acceleration.

Operating Principle

  • Forward stroke: slow, inclined — material moves forward with trough
  • Return stroke: fast — material separates from trough surface briefly (hops forward)
  • Net displacement per cycle: forward; frequency typically 15–60 Hz

Advantages in Pharmacy

  • No moving parts contacting material — easy cleaning
  • Can convey hot, sticky, or abrasive materials
  • Gentle on fragile tablets and coated pellets
  • Can be totally enclosed for dust control
  • Used as tablet de-duster and metal detector conveyor

Key Parameters

  • Amplitude: 2–10 mm; Frequency: 15–60 Hz
  • Throw angle: 15–30° from horizontal
  • Speed depends on amplitude × frequency
