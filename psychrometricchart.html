Psychrometric Chart

A graphical representation of thermodynamic properties of moist air — relating dry-bulb temperature, humidity ratio, relative humidity, enthalpy, wet-bulb temperature, and dew point.

Key Properties

  • Dry-bulb temperature (DBT): actual air temperature on x-axis
  • Humidity ratio (W): kg water vapour per kg dry air (y-axis)
  • Relative humidity (RH): curved lines from 10% to 100% (saturation curve)
  • Wet-bulb temperature: diagonal lines (adiabatic saturation lines)
  • Dew point: temperature at which air becomes saturated
  • Enthalpy (h): total energy content of moist air

Air Conditioning Processes on Chart

  • Sensible heating/cooling: horizontal line (W constant, DBT changes)
  • Humidification: vertical line (DBT constant, W increases)
  • Cooling + dehumidification: follows to saturation curve, then along it
  • Adiabatic humidification: along wet-bulb temperature line
