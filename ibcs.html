IBCs — Intermediate Bulk Containers & Bin Blending

Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBCs)

IBCs are rigid, portable stainless steel containers (50–2000 L) used to store and transfer bulk pharmaceutical powders and granules between manufacturing stages while maintaining containment.

IBC Bin Blender

Rather than transferring powder into a separate blender, the IBC itself is mounted on a drive frame and rotated (tumbled) to blend contents inside. This is contained blending — no powder transfer needed.

Key Advantages

  • Zero product transfer = zero contamination risk and no yield loss
  • Full containment — critical for potent/high-hazard APIs (OEB 4–5)
  • Validated cleaning-in-place (WIP/CIP compatible)
  • Direct docking to tablet press via gravity discharge valve
  • Full electronic batch record — weight, rotations, time

IBC Blending Parameters

  • Speed: 4–25 RPM (slower for fragile granules)
  • Fill level: 60–80% capacity
  • Number of rotations: 50–500 (validated per product)
  • Baffles inside IBC enhance mixing efficiency
