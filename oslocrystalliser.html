What you are seeing

Supersaturated solution circulates UPWARD through the fluidised crystal bed in the growth chamber (centre tall vessel). The circulator pump (bottom) draws solution from below, passes it through the heat exchanger (left) to create supersaturation, then pumps it up through the crystal bed. Crystals grow steadily. Oversized crystals sink and are discharged at the bottom.

Key Point: The Oslo Crystalliser separates NUCLEATION (in the circulator pipe — high supersaturation) from GROWTH (in the growth chamber — controlled low supersaturation). Result: fewer nuclei, larger crystals — superior to Swenson-Walker for crystal size.

Oslo Crystalliser — Theory

  • Growth chamber: Tall vertical vessel containing fluidised crystal bed — low supersaturation zone, crystals grow undisturbed
  • Circulator: Pump circulates solution from growth chamber bottom, through heat exchanger, back up through crystal bed
  • Heat exchanger: Cools circulating solution — creates supersaturation just above metastable limit
  • Crystal discharge: Large crystals settle against upward flow, removed from conical bottom
  • Result: Crystal size 2-5 mm, much larger than Swenson-Walker (0.5-1 mm)
Key Fact: Oslo crystalliser also called Krystal or growth-type crystalliser. Used for potassium chloride, ammonium sulphate, sodium chloride, citric acid — wherever large crystal size is needed for easy filtration and high purity.
Q1. The Oslo Crystalliser produces larger crystals than the Swenson-Walker because:
Correct. In Oslo, the growth chamber supersaturation is maintained just above the metastable zone limit — enough to deposit solute onto existing crystals but insufficient to create new nuclei. Result: fewer, larger, more uniform crystals that are easier to filter and wash.
Q2. Large crystals in the Oslo growth chamber are discharged by:
Correct. The upward liquid velocity is set to fluidise crystals of target size. Crystals larger than target size have settling velocity greater than upward liquid velocity — they sink through the bed and are discharged. This creates natural size classification — largest crystals at bottom, smallest at top of bed.
