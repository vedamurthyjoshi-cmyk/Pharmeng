Pneumatic Conveying — Dilute Phase vs Dense Phase

Pneumatic Conveying

Bulk powders and granules are transported through enclosed pipelines using air pressure (positive) or vacuum (negative). Two main modes exist:

Dilute Phase (Suspension Flow)

  • High air velocity (15–35 m/s); particles fully suspended in air stream
  • Low material-to-air ratio (loading ratio < 15)
  • Used for light, fine powders over long distances
  • Risk of particle attrition and segregation

Dense Phase (Plug/Slug Flow)

  • Low air velocity (1–5 m/s); material moves in plugs or slugs
  • High loading ratio (> 15); uses pulse air injection
  • Gentle on fragile particles (pellets, coated tablets)
  • Preferred for pharmaceutical API transfer

Key Components

  • Blower/compressor — generates positive pressure air
  • Rotary airlock valve — feeds material without air leakage
  • Pipeline — stainless steel, sanitary fittings
  • Cyclone or bag filter — separates material from air at destination
