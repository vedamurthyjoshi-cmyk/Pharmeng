💡 What you are seeing

Two sigma (Σ-shaped) blades rotate toward each other at slightly different speeds (typically 2:3 ratio) inside a W-shaped trough. Material is squeezed, stretched, and folded between the blades — intense kneading and shear. This action is essential for viscous, cohesive materials that cannot be mixed by tumbling or ribbon blenders.

🔑 The Sigma Blade Mixer is the standard mixer for high-viscosity pharmaceutical preparations — wet granulation masses, rubber-based preparations, ointments, and suppository bases. The two blades at different speeds create differential shear — more intensive than same-speed mixing.

Principle

Two sigma (Σ) shaped blades rotate in opposite directions at slightly different speeds (front blade slightly faster) inside a W-shaped (double-trough) bowl. Material is caught between the blades and intensively kneaded by compression, stretching, and folding — high-shear mixing. Suitable for viscous, sticky, and cohesive materials.

Construction

  • Bowl: W-shaped (two semi-circular troughs joined) — matches the two sigma blade profile
  • Sigma blades: Two Σ-shaped blades; each fits closely in one half of the W-trough; counter-rotating; front blade faster (ratio 3:2 or 2:1)
  • Jacketed bowl (optional): Steam or water jacket for temperature control during mixing
  • Tilting mechanism: Entire mixer bowl tilts for discharge of viscous product
  • Speed: 15–60 rpm; heavy-duty motor for high-viscosity materials

Pharmaceutical Applications

  • Wet granulation of high-viscosity granulation masses
  • Manufacturing rubber-based pharmaceutical preparations
  • Mixing of suppository bases (theobroma oil, PEG bases) with drugs
  • Ointment manufacturing — especially thick, stiff ointments
  • Industrial: manufacturing of chewing gum bases, adhesive plasters, medicated gums
🔑 The key characteristic of Sigma Blade Mixer: handles materials too viscous and cohesive for ANY other pharmaceutical mixer — if the material does not flow, the sigma blade can still knead it.
Q1. Why do the two sigma blades in a Sigma Blade Mixer rotate at different speeds?
✅ Differential speed mechanics: with blades at ratio 2:1 (front:rear), material caught between them experiences relative velocity difference — one surface moves faster than the other → velocity gradient → shear stress across material thickness → stretching and folding. This is more effective than equal-speed counter-rotation (which simply squeezes without velocity gradient). The differential creates a more thorough kneading action similar to manual bread kneading between palms moving at different speeds.
Q2. The Sigma Blade Mixer is chosen over a Ribbon Blender for pharmaceutical ointment manufacturing because:
✅ Viscosity limit of ribbon blender: ribbons work by moving material through the trough — requires material to flow (viscosity < ~50,000 mPa·s practical limit). Ointments, suppository masses, rubber preparations: viscosity 100,000+ mPa·s — will not flow at all. Sigma blades: direct mechanical contact with material → force applied regardless of viscosity → kneads material by deformation, not flow. This is why sigma mixers are called "kneaders" rather than "blenders."
