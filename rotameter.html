What you are seeing

A FLOAT rises inside a vertically mounted TAPERED tube (wider at top). As flow increases from below, the upward drag force on the float increases. The float rises until the annular area between float and tube wall is large enough that drag force equals float weight. The float position on the calibrated scale indicates flow rate directly -- no external transmitter needed.

Key Point: Rotameter is a variable-area, constant pressure drop meter -- unlike Venturimeter (constant area, variable pressure drop). Flow rate READ directly from float position -- simple, robust, no power required. The only moving part is the float.

Rotameter (Variable Area Meter) -- Theory

  • Float: Dense material (SS, glass, sapphire) shaped to rotate for stability (hence ro-tameter); various shapes -- sharp-edged disc, domed, sphere
  • Tube: Glass (see-through for small flows) or metal (with magnetic follower for high pressure/temperature)
  • Calibration: Scale engraved on tube or reference plate; specific to one fluid and one set of conditions
  • Range: 5:1 or 10:1 turndown ratio
  • Pharmaceutical use: Oxygen flow in fermenters; nitrogen purge flow; WFI flows up to 10 L/min; gas flow meters in HVAC

Q1. The float in a Rotameter rises higher when flow increases because:
Correct. Equilibrium condition: F_drag = W_float (minus buoyancy). F_drag = Cd x A_annular x (0.5 x rho x v^2). As Q increases, v increases (assuming fixed annular area) so F_drag increases above W -- float rises -- A_annular increases -- v decreases -- new equilibrium. At equilibrium for any flow rate: A_annular x v = Q/A_float (constant relationship) -- gives proportional scale.
Q2. Rotameter calibration must be repeated if:
Correct. Float equilibrium: W_float - F_buoyancy = F_drag. F_buoyancy = rho_fluid x V_float x g -- changes with fluid density. F_drag = Cd x A_annular x 0.5 x rho_fluid x v^2 -- also changes with fluid density and viscosity (affects Cd). If rho_fluid changes (e.g., from water to glycerol solution), both buoyancy and drag change -- float equilibrium at different height for same flow -- incorrect reading. Must recalibrate or apply correction factor: Q_actual = Q_reading x sqrt((rho_float - rho_cal)/(rho_float - rho_actual)).
