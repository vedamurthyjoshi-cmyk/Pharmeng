Desiccant Dehumidifier (Rotary Wheel)

A large rotating wheel impregnated with silica gel or molecular sieve adsorbs moisture from process air in the adsorption zone. The saturated wheel continuously rotates into the reactivation zone where hot air drives off moisture, regenerating the desiccant.

Key Zones

  • Adsorption zone (process side ~75%): Moist process air passes through; moisture adsorbed onto desiccant
  • Reactivation zone (regen side ~25%): Hot air (120–140°C) drives off moisture; exhausted outside
  • Cooling sector: Wheel pre-cooled before entering adsorption zone

Advantages over Cooling Coil

  • Can achieve very low dew points (< −40°C) — critical for hygroscopic APIs
  • Works independently of ambient temperature
  • Continuous operation — no defrost cycle needed

Pharmaceutical Use

  • Granulation rooms for moisture-sensitive APIs
  • Tablet compression and coating suites
  • Lyophilisation (freeze-drying) ancillary areas
  • Cold store dehumidification
