💡 What you are seeing

Two heavy granite or steel wheels (runners) roll around a circular pan. The material sits in the pan and is crushed under the enormous weight of the wheels as they pass over it. The wheel also slides slightly sideways — providing attrition (shear) in addition to compression. A scraper blade continuously moves material back under the wheels.

🔑 The Edge Runner Mill works by compression + attrition. The wheel weight provides compression; the slight sideways sliding of the wheel provides shear/attrition. Produces 50–1000 µm particle size. Ideal for soft to moderately hard materials.

Principle

The Edge Runner Mill (also called Chilean Mill or Roller Mill) consists of two heavy cylindrical wheels (runners) mounted on a horizontal shaft that rotates about a vertical central axis. The wheels roll around the inside of a circular pan, crushing and grinding material by compression (weight of wheel) and attrition (slight lateral sliding). A scraper continuously pushes material back under the wheels.

Construction

  • Pan: Circular flat-bottomed pan, 0.5–2 m diameter; granite, steel, or cast iron
  • Runners (wheels): Two heavy cylindrical wheels; granite or steel; rotate freely on axle; also rotate about central vertical shaft
  • Central drive shaft: Vertical shaft rotates at 15–30 rpm; carries the wheel axle around the pan
  • Scraper blades: Fixed blades push material from pan walls and floor back under wheel path
  • Screen (optional): Perforated plate at bottom for continuous discharge when product is fine enough

Applications

  • Grinding of soft to moderately hard materials — beeswax, resins, crude drugs
  • Preparation of ointments — grinding and mixing simultaneously
  • Grinding of damp or moist materials (not suitable for dry fine powders)
  • Milling of clays, pigments, and colour bases for pharmaceutical preparations
🔑 Edge Runner Mill is unique in that it can handle both dry and moist/pasty materials — and it mixes while it grinds. This makes it suitable for ointment manufacturing where drug must be intimately mixed with the base.

Advantages vs Disadvantages

  • ✅ Handles moist, pasty, waxy materials — other mills cannot
  • ✅ Simultaneous grinding and mixing
  • ✅ Low speed — less heat generated than hammer mill
  • ✅ Simple, robust construction
  • ❌ Slow throughput; batch operation
  • ❌ Not suitable for very hard materials
  • ❌ Difficult to clean — open pan design
  • ❌ Cannot produce very fine powder (<50 µm)
Q1. The size reduction mechanism in an Edge Runner Mill is:
✅ Edge Runner = Compression + Attrition. Compression: each runner weighs 1–5 tonnes → enormous pressure on particles beneath → crushing. Attrition: the runner does not roll perfectly — it slides slightly sideways as it goes around the pan → shear force → attrition. Compare: Ball Mill (impact + attrition), Hammer Mill (impact), Colloid Mill (shear only). The dual mechanism makes Edge Runner versatile for different material types.
Q2. The Edge Runner Mill is particularly suitable for ointment manufacturing because:
✅ Ointment manufacturing application: drug particles + ointment base loaded into pan → runners roll → drug crushed to fine particles AND simultaneously mixed/dispersed into the semisolid base. No other single mill achieves this for semisolid formulations. Alternative: triple roller mill (three rollers in sequence) — also used for ointments, provides more controlled shear. Both work on compression + attrition principle.
Q3. The purpose of the scraper blades in an Edge Runner Mill is:
✅ Scraper function: as the heavy runner rolls over material, it pushes some material sideways toward the pan edges and walls. Without scrapers, this material would pile up at the periphery and never be ground. Scrapers (fixed blades attached to the central shaft) redirect material back under the wheel path → every portion of material gets processed → uniform grinding. Some designs also have a central scraper to prevent dead zones near the shaft.
