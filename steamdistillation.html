Steam Distillation

Steam distillation is used to separate heat-sensitive, water-immiscible substances (like essential oils) at temperatures below their normal boiling point.

  • Principle: Total vapour pressure = P(steam) + P(organic). Mixture boils when total P = atmospheric
  • Advantage: Distillation occurs well below 100 °C — prevents decomposition
  • Pharmaceutical use: Isolation of volatile oils (peppermint, clove, eucalyptus)
  • Distillate layers: Organic layer (oil) floats on water layer — separated by separating funnel
  • Steam generator: External boiler supplies steam directly into the distillation flask
