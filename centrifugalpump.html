What you are seeing

An impeller with curved vanes rotates at high speed inside a volute casing. Liquid enters at the impeller EYE (centre) and is thrown outward by centrifugal force into the volute. As liquid decelerates in the widening volute, kinetic energy converts to pressure energy (Bernoulli). High-pressure liquid exits through the discharge nozzle.

Key Point: The centrifugal pump converts mechanical rotation (kinetic energy) into fluid pressure (potential energy). Flow rate adjustable by valve or VFD (variable frequency drive). Cannot pump gases or vapour -- loses prime if suction side contains air.

Centrifugal Pump -- Theory

  • Impeller: 2-6 backward-curved vanes; SS 316L for pharmaceutical service; 1000-3500 rpm
  • Volute: Spiral casing that decelerates fluid and converts velocity to pressure
  • Shaft seal: Mechanical seal prevents liquid leaking along shaft; single or double seal for pharmaceutical
  • Sanitary design: No dead legs; Ra less than 0.8 micrometres; crevice-free; CIP/SIP compatible; USP Class VI seals
  • Use: Transfer of WFI, process water, buffer solutions, cleaning agents in pharmaceutical plants

Q1. The centrifugal pump raises liquid pressure by:
Correct. Bernoulli equation: P + 0.5*rho*v^2 = constant. In impeller: low P, high v (liquid thrown outward fast). In volute: v decreases as cross-section widens, so P increases. Pump head H = v^2/(2g) converted to pressure. Centrifugal pump characteristic: at zero flow, max head; at max flow, zero head -- operation point where pump curve meets system curve.
Q2. A centrifugal pump in pharmaceutical WFI distribution operates with a variable frequency drive (VFD) rather than a control valve because:
Correct. Affinity laws: Q proportional to N (speed); H proportional to N^2; Power proportional to N^3. Reducing pump speed by 20% reduces power by 49% -- huge energy saving vs throttling valve (which wastes energy as heat across the valve). Also: no deadleg in valve body (hygienic concern in WFI systems); smoother pressure control; reduced wear on impeller and seals at lower speed.
