Screw Conveyor (Archimedes Screw)

A rotating helical screw inside a trough or tube conveys bulk materials axially. The screw blade (flight) pushes material forward as it rotates.

Key Components

  • Central shaft: rotates; driven by motor at feed end
  • Helical flight: continuous spiral blade welded to shaft
  • Trough: U-shaped or tubular housing; may be enclosed
  • Feed hopper: inlet where powder/granules enter
  • Discharge: outlet at opposite end; can be bottom or end

Capacity Formula

Q = (π/4)(D² − d²) × pitch × N × ρ × fill factor

D = outer diameter, d = shaft diameter, N = RPM, ρ = bulk density

Pharmaceutical Use

  • Conveying granules from granulator to dryer
  • Feeding powder to tablet press hopper
  • Transferring API from bulk drum to blender
  • Enclosed design prevents contamination and dust
